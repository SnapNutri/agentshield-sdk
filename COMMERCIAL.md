# Commercial Model

AgentShield's core SDK is free and open source under the MIT License. This
repository does not currently include a paid feature gate, hosted service,
payment integration, or staffed support operation.

## Potential Paid Offerings

Subject to a separate agreement and actual availability, professional work
could include:

- AgentShield integration and migration assistance
- Runtime protection policy design and tuning
- AI-agent safety and architecture reviews
- Priority support for a defined project
- Custom integrations and private deployment assistance
- Enterprise-oriented documentation and onboarding

## Future Product Directions

Advanced policy templates, reporting, analytics, and hosted monitoring could
be developed as paid offerings only after the requirements are validated with
real users. They are not implemented or sold by this repository today.

## First-Customer Path

The simplest realistic path is to keep the SDK free, publish a clear service
scope, and speak with early adopters about a paid integration or policy-review
engagement. Pricing, contracting, payment collection, and customer support
require human business-account setup and are intentionally not fabricated here.
